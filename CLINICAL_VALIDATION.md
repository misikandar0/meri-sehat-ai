# Meri Sehat AI — Clinical Validation Checklist & Roadmap

## 1. Clinical Design Principles
1. **Educational Only:** Meri Sehat does not provide conclusive medical diagnoses or initiate/stop drug treatments.
2. **Contextual Pattern Recognition:** Rather than flagging single numbers in isolation, the platform correlates multi-marker patterns (e.g. Microcytic Hypochromic Anemia, Reactive Leukocytosis).
3. **Restrained Visualization:** Avoids alarmist red colors for minor, routine physiological variations.
4. **Mandatory Urgent Escalation:** Immediately highlights critical laboratory flags (e.g. Platelets < 50,000 / µL) and instructs patients to seek urgent hospital emergency care (Rescue 1122).

## 2. Implemented Laboratory Panels (Phase 1)
- **Complete Blood Count (CBC):**
  - Hemoglobin (Hb / HGB)
  - Red Blood Cell Count (RBC)
  - Mean Corpuscular Volume (MCV)
  - White Blood Cell Count (WBC / TLC)
  - Platelet Count (PLT)
- **Basic Metabolic & Glucose:**
  - Fasting Blood Glucose (FBS)

## 3. Future Roadmap (Phases 2 & 3)
Following specialized hematology and pathology peer-review:
- Serum Ferritin & Iron Studies (TIBC, Transferrin Saturation)
- HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin)
- Lipid Profile (Total Cholesterol, LDL, HDL, Triglycerides)
- Liver Function Tests (ALT, AST, Bilirubin, Alkaline Phosphatase)
- Renal Function Tests (Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea Nitrogen, eGFR)
- Thyroid Function Tests (TSH, Free T3, Free T4)

## 4. Pakistan Medical Context
- **Dengue Fever Seasonal Outbreaks:** Severe thrombocytopenia is a primary public health risk in major cities (Lahore, Karachi, Rawalpindi). Immediate clinical triage with blood smear confirmation is prioritized.
- **Thalassemia Trait (Minor):** Highly prevalent genetic carrier state in South Asia producing microcytosis. Patients are directed to discuss Serum Ferritin and Hemoglobin Electrophoresis rather than assuming iron deficiency.
